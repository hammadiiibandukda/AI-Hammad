Questions about installing

Do I need both the app and the extension?
No, but most people use both. The extension is where you create identities and autofill them while you browse on a computer; the app is where you manage calls, texts, and alerts on your phone. Everything syncs to the same account.
Which browsers does the extension support?
Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Arc. They all install the same extension from the Chrome Web Store. There is no Firefox or Safari extension today — on those browsers, use the web app.
Can I use Cloaked without installing anything?
Yes. The web app gives you your full account in any browser — identities, passwords, data removal status, and alerts.
Is there a desktop app for macOS or Windows?
Not a native desktop app. On a computer, Cloaked runs as a browser extension plus the web app, which together cover signing up, autofill, and account management.
Do I pay separately for each platform?
No. One subscription covers every platform, and you can be signed in on all of them at once. See plans and pricing.
